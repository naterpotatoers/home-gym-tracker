# Review rubric

Run every section for each program, then again for each person against what
they actually did (digest "What they actually did"). Thresholds are starting
points for judgment, not pass/fail gates. Lens/evidence for each is in
[coaching-lens.md](coaching-lens.md).

## 1. Fit to the real schedule (check first; it changes everything else)

- Planned days/week (program week layout) vs actual sessions/week and which
  weekdays (digest per-week + training-days lines).
- Which routines actually get run. A weekday-locked A/B/C plan run 1–2×/week
  silently becomes "A every time" (the launcher pre-selects today's program
  day by weekday, otherwise the routine done last — see app-mapping).
- Is the assignment active and inside its length? An ended or completed
  assignment means the person is training off-program.
- **Design rule:** 1 day/week → one self-sufficient session; 2 days → two
  stand-alone A/B days, either order; 3+ → splits are fine. If they don't
  train on fixed weekdays, prefer stand-alone routines over a sequence that
  breaks when a day is missed.

## 2. Volume and coverage

Use the app's weighted sets (sets × score/10; neglected <2 or peak <5,
light <6, solid 6+).

| Priority | Weekly weighted sets | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Goal muscles | 10–20 | growth; more isn't better past ~20 for most |
| Everything else | 4–6 | maintenance is cheap at the same intensity |
| Per session, one muscle | ≲ 6–10 | beyond this, move sets to another day or cut |

- Flag: a goal muscle that's light or neglected in **logged** coverage (not
  just prescribed), or one muscle over ~10 in a single session.
- Glutes/hamstrings for a glute goal: cover short-length hip extension, a
  long-length hinge, and deep knee-dominant single-leg work.
- Mobility and conditioning rows don't count toward volume (by design).

## 3. Pattern balance

From the digest's pattern-sets line (catalog patterns: `squat, hinge, lunge,
push_h, push_v, pull_h, pull_v, core, carry, isolation, mobility,
conditioning`):

- Every week (every session for 1×/week people) touches squat or lunge,
  hinge, a push, a pull, and trunk.
- **Pull sets ≥ push sets**; aim ~1.5:1 for desk-bound people. Each vertical
  push should have a vertical pull somewhere in the week (`pull_v` = 0 is a
  finding).
- Trunk work covers anti-extension, anti-rotation and lateral (plank/dead
  bug, Pallof, side plank/suitcase carry), not only flexion.
- Carries and single-leg work appear for anyone over ~50, any athlete, and any
  runner.

## 4. Prescription

- **Rep ranges must allow double progression:** a range of at least 2 reps,
  and 3–4+ for fixed dumbbells (5 lb a hand is +33% on a 15 lb bell). A
  fixed target ("3×10" with min = max) leaves no way to progress except load
  jumps people won't take; digest stalls usually trace here.
- Typical ranges: heavy compounds 4–8; hypertrophy compounds 6–12;
  accessories/isolation 10–20; glute-med/cuff 12–20; timed holds per the
  care docs.
- **RIR:** compounds 1–3 (beginners 2–3; technical barbell lifts are never to
  failure); isolation 0–2; care/mobility = quality reps, RIR null.
- **Rest:** heavy compounds 150–210 s; moderate compounds 90–120 s; isolation
  45–75 s. One rest value for every row is a finding.
- **Order:** technical/heavy first, then other compounds, unilateral, then
  isolation and trunk. Don't fatigue the trunk or grip before heavy
  squats/deadlifts.
- **Session budget:** ≲ 60–75 min and ~16–24 working sets (beginners at the
  low end). The digest's "est. ~N min" plus the logged median duration tell
  you if a session is too long; long sessions get the end skipped (check
  "prescribed but not done").

## 5. Progression and structure

- Each main lift states its rule in the row notes ("add 5 lb when all sets
  hit the top of the range at the target RIR").
- Load steps are realistic for the equipment (barbell 2.5 lb, dumbbells 5 lb
  a hand, bands ordinal).
- Stall plan: 3+ exposures flat → change the rep range, swap variant, or
  reduce sets for a week. Digest "STALLED" flags are candidates; look at
  whether the person was even prescribed a way to progress.
- Blocks of 4–8 weeks; re-review at the end; re-assign when an assignment
  ends.

## 6. PT lens (pain, joints, special populations)

- Every ⚠ note in the digest gets a response: what movement, a regression
  (range, tempo, load, variant), a return-to-load path, and red flags →
  clinician. Use the pain-monitoring rule (≤5/10, settled by next morning).
- Joint-friendly selection: pressing has a pulling counterweight; knees get
  hip + quad work; low backs get hinge practice plus anti-extension trunk
  work, not loaded flexion when symptomatic.
- Mobility gaps behind recurring faults (ankle dorsiflexion for squats and
  split squats, T-spine for overhead) get a prep drill in the warm-up.
- **Beginners:** fewer exercises (5–7), master patterns, RIR 2–3, simple
  progression, full body 2–3×/week if they'll come that often.
- **60+:** balance, power (fast concentric on easy loads), carries,
  sit-to-stand/step-ups, progressive loading for bone; technique first.
- **Fat-loss goal:** resistance training preserves lean mass during a deficit;
  add conditioning (Echo Bike pieces in the routine) and point at the
  nutrition tracking. Weekly weigh-ins are in the digest.

## 7. Runners (concurrent training)

- Hardest lower-body session ≥48 h before the long run or key session; the
  day before the long run stays sub-maximal.
- Strength 2×/week minimum for the legs, heavy enough to matter.
- Calves and soleus trained heavy, straight- and bent-knee; tibialis and
  hamstring (Nordic) prehab present.
- Plyometrics/elastic work 1–2×/week, low volume (pogo hops, skips, bounds),
  for running economy.
- Glute med and adductors present (side-lying abduction, Copenhagen).
- Mileage: planned vs actual from the digest; no >30% jumps over two weeks;
  the long run shouldn't dominate the week (≲ 30–40% of weekly miles
  [consensus]); most runs conversational.

## 8. Reporting checklist

- [ ] Headline: 3–5 bullets, most important first.
- [ ] Per program: what's working, then findings by severity.
- [ ] Per person: intent → plan → reality, with digest numbers.
- [ ] Every change written as builder rows.
- [ ] Pain notes answered, with referral language where warranted.
- [ ] Evidence tier on every finding; influence vs evidence conflicts named.
