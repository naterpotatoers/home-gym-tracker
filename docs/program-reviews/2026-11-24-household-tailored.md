# Program review — tailored household programs, 2026-11-24

Follow-up to `2026-11-17-household.md`. Coach + PT review made with the
`program-review` skill; numbers come from its digest of the live data
(16 weeks, Aug 10 – Nov 24) and coverage uses the app's own math ("weighted
sets" = sets × muscle score / 10). Lens: Squat University, Jonni Shreve,
AthleanX, each checked against the evidence (tiers in brackets).

> Not medical advice. Pain is coached around with a pain-monitoring rule;
> red flags go to a clinician.

## Headline

1. **Everyone was on one routine.** On 11-23 Nate, Gabe, Lidia and Arnold all
   ran Nate's *Lift A*. It had been softened for the family (band-assisted
   pull-ups), and they skipped what didn't fit (Nordics, pull-ups). Each
   person now has their own program.
2. **Starter can't progress.** Every row is a fixed 3×10 with no rule for
   adding load, there is no vertical pull, and push:pull is 15:9. Lidia and
   Gabe were re-assigned to it on 11-24; both move off it.
3. **The weekday programs share one structure** so three people fit one rack,
   one bench and one bike: the brothers share the bar on the same lift, Lidia
   works opposite them, and the bike is taken in turns.
4. **The warm-up is two things: the 4:00 easy bike and ramp sets on the first
   lift.** That is all a solo session needs.
5. **Buy a pair of 1.25 lb plates.** The smallest step on the bar is 5 lb
   today. That is a 15% jump on Lidia's press, which is why it has sat at the
   empty bar for 11 sessions.

Two things this review does not treat as problems. Sessions run 77–89 min
(logged medians) mostly because rest is loose, and that is fine; the plans
aim for about 60 min by prescribing fewer sets, not by policing rest. And Gabe
and Nate are coming back from a 1–2 month layoff, so their flat numbers are an
ease-back, not a stall.

## How the sessions fit together

### The warm-up

| Part | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| General | Echo Bike, 4:00 easy (8×20/10, RPE 3–4) | Raises temperature and heart rate. Already in the app. |
| Specific | Ramp sets on the first lift: bar × 8, about half the working weight × 5, about three-quarters × 3 | Rehearses the pattern under rising load. Log them with "Make warm-up". |

Mobility drills are not a required third part [consensus]. They are in a
routine only where they are training for that person.

### One bike

| People | Nate (rides 1st) | Gabe (2nd) | Lidia (3rd) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo | Bike → ramp sets | | |
| Two | Bike → ramp sets | Empty-bar ramp sets → bike | |
| Three | Bike → ramp sets | Empty-bar ramp sets → bike | Her two balance rows → bike |

- Nate rides first because his ramp to 190 takes longest.
- Gabe has no prep rows. The rack is free while Nate rides, so he starts
  ramping.
- Lidia opens with two short rows (single-leg balance, fast sit-to-stand).
  They are her fall-prevention work; solo she can do them after the bike.
- Nate has two short runner rows per day (jumps, ankle, glute med, tibialis).
  Optional when solo.
- The app still puts the bike first on every card. Riders 2 and 3 tap another
  block first; the runner allows that.
- A fourth person (Vivica on Mondays) starts her own two prep rows and rides
  when the bike is free. If someone is absent, move up a slot.

first, so only the outer plates come off between turns. Lidia is on dumbbells
or the floor while they have the rack.

## Nate — Lift 3 + Run 3 (week 3 of 6)

**Intent:** overall strength, with running and staying uninjured.
**Reality:** 24 lifting sessions in 16 weeks (1.5/week, 3 a week lately);
24 runs, 7.7 mi/week at 1.5 runs/week against about 16.5 mi planned.

**Working:** heavy squat Monday, the only heavy hinge Wednesday, sub-maximal
single-leg work Friday before the long run [SR/MA — Wilson 2012; consensus].
Nordics, Copenhagens and tibialis raises are in. Push:pull is 9:14.

| Severity | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Should change | The 50 lb dumbbells are too light for rows: 50×12 on a 3×8–10 chest-supported row, 50×10 on the Monday row. | Monday row → barbell 3×6–10. Wednesday row → 3×10–15 with a 2 s pause. |
| Should change | Pull-ups were set to band-assisted for the family; a pull-up note was left on the bent-over-row row. | Bodyweight pull-ups 3×6–10, then weighted. Note fixed. |
| Should change | Calves at 4.8 weighted sets/week, all straight-knee. Soleus capacity matters for running load [consensus, mechanistic]. | Wednesday: bent-knee single-leg calf raise 3×10–15. Calves → 9.6. |
| Should change | Friday's split squat and single-leg RDL are a fixed 3×8, so the only way up is a 20% dumbbell jump. | 3×8–10; all sets at 10 → next pair. RIR 3 stays. |
| Should change | Running is under half the plan and uneven (weeks of 3.6 and 11.6 mi back to back). Jumps over 30% in two weeks raise injury risk in novices [cohort — Nielsen 2014]. | Program notes: Tue 3, Thu 3, Sat 5 (11 mi), then +1 mi/week on the long run only, hold every third week. |
| Consider | No plyometrics. Heavy strength plus plyometrics improves running economy [SR/MA — Balsalobre-Fernández 2016; Blagrove 2018]. | Monday: squat jumps 3×3 before squats. Swap to pogo hops once they're in the catalog. |
| Consider | Glute med 5.3 weighted sets/week [case-control — Fredericson 2000 for runners with ITB pain]. | Wednesday lateral walk 2×10, Friday side-lying abduction 2×12–20. Glute med → 9.3. |
| Consider | Bench, press and incline rows had no progression note. | Notes added; press range widened to 5–8 because the bar steps 5 lb. |
| Consider | TKEs, a quad stretch and knee-to-wall were added by hand on 11-13 and 11-16. | If a knee is bothering you, say so and it gets its own plan. Pain past 5/10, or not settled by next morning, means back off [RCT — Silbernagel 2007]. |

Week after the changes (app math): quads 12.5, hamstrings 10.3, glutes 15.4,
glute med 9.3, adductors 7.3, calves 9.6 — all solid. Push:pull 9:14. Days are
47 / 48 / 52 min at the prescribed rests, before the bike.

## Gabe — Base Strength — 3 Day (new, 8 weeks)

**Intent:** get stronger and lose weight; newer lifter (31, 210 lb).
**Reality:** 9 sessions in 15 weeks with a layoff from late September to
early November; Monday and Wednesday lately. Five of nine sessions were the
Starter squat day. Recent top sets: squat 75×10, bench 65×10, deadlift 75×10.

| Severity | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Fix now | On Starter again (or borrowing Lift A): fixed 3×10, no rule for adding load. | His own program, full body every day so a 1–2 day week still covers everything. |
| Should change | Dumbbell presses jump 25% (20 → 25 lb a hand). | Barbell overhead press from the empty bar; 5 lb steps. |
| Should change | No vertical pull in Starter. | Band-assisted chin-ups Monday. |
| Should change | Beginners do best with few lifts and a simple rule [consensus; rubric]. | Six exercises and 16 sets a day. All sets at the top of the range → +5 lb. |
| Consider | Fat-loss goal: lifting preserves muscle in a deficit; the loss itself is mostly food. | Optional Echo Bike finisher; keep the weekly weigh-in (210 lb on 11-11). |

Weeks 1–2 are the ease-back: recent loads, 3 reps in reserve. Week (app
math): quads 9.3, hamstrings 9.3, glutes 14.0, lats 9.3 — solid. Push:pull
9:11, one vertical pull. Days are 32–36 min before the bike.

If he comes only on Mondays he only ever gets day A. The launcher lists B and
C under his program; pick one by hand on those weeks.

## Lidia — Strong & Steady — 3 Day (new, 8 weeks)

**Intent:** fat loss, and training she enjoys at 64. No barbell hip thrusts,
dips or pull-ups. Likes face pulls.
**Reality:** the most consistent person in the gym: 24 sessions in 16 weeks,
3 a week lately. Bench 33 → 50, squat 10 → 50, deadlift 53 → 58,
single-arm row 20 → 25. Press flat at 33×10 for 11 sessions.

| Severity | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Fix now | On Starter again: fixed 3×10 and 27–30 sets a day. She skipped hip thrusts 4 times. | Her own program; banded glute bridges replace hip thrusts. |
| Should change | No balance, power or carry work. Balance and functional exercise cut the rate of falls by about a quarter [SR/MA — Sherrington 2019]. | Every day opens with single-leg standing hip abduction and a fast sit-to-stand. Farmer carries Wednesday; step-ups and reverse lunges in the week. |
| Should change | Loads should drift heavier over time for bone [RCT — Watson 2018 LIFTMOR, a supervised program]. | Squat, bench and deadlift move from 3×10 to 6–10 and 6–8. Technique first; never grind. |
| Should change | Press stuck at the empty Bella bar: the next loadable weight is 38 lb (+15%). | Seated dumbbell press 3×8–12 so reps can climb first. The 1.25 lb plates fix the barbell version. |
| Should change | No vertical pull. | Dumbbell pullover on Friday as a stand-in. Better: add **Band Lat Pulldown** to the catalog. |
| Consider | Lateral raises went 5 lb → 3 lb. | Dropped; pressing and face pulls cover the shoulder. |

Face pulls are in all three days. Week (app math): quads 16.5, hamstrings
12.6, glutes 21.3, glute med 9.4, rear delts 10.3, lats 8.0 — solid; the leg
numbers include six bodyweight sit-to-stand sets. Push:pull 9:15. Days are
43–47 min before the bike.

## Arnold — Foundations — Saturday (new, 8 weeks)

**Intent:** fat loss; 58, 285 lb, injury-free, about one day a week
(Saturday). Nothing lying face-down. Keep it easy until he's consistent.
**Reality:** 4 sessions in 16 weeks, on three different routines.

One self-sufficient session: squat to the bench, bench press, a standing
single-arm row, a light dumbbell RDL, face pull + Pallof press, and a farmer
carry. 17 sets, about 36 min before the bike, 3 reps in reserve throughout.
One session a week needs every pattern in it [SR/MA — Schoenfeld 2019 on
frequency; rubric]. Push:pull 3:5.

The bike stays easy (RPE 3–4). Chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness
means stop and see a doctor.
